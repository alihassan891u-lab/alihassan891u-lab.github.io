FAQ

Before we start

Short answers to what clients ask most. Anything else, just ask.

Ask a question
What do you need from me to start?

A short description of the task, the tools you already use and one real example of the input: a document, a call recording, a spreadsheet or a ticket. I reply with a plan, the simplest approach that will work, and a fixed price or an hourly estimate.

How do you prove the AI actually works?

Before launch I test the system on examples it never saw while it was being built, and you get the numbers: accuracy, failure cases, speed and cost per run. You see the same report I see.

Can an AI agent change things in our systems safely?

Yes, with limits enforced in code, not just in the prompt: read-only access by default, a preview and your approval before anything changes, and an audit log of every action the agent takes.

Do you work with our existing stack?

Usually. I work with OpenAI, Claude, open-source models and AWS Bedrock, and connect them to CRMs, help desks, databases, calendars and internal APIs. If a tool has an API, an agent can use it.

What happens after launch?

You get the full code, documentation and a handover call. I can stay on for monitoring, improvements and new features, or hand everything to your team.
